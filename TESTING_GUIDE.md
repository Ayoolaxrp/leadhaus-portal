# How to test the Leadhaus portal

No technical knowledge needed. Follow the steps in order and tick each box. If something does not happen the way it says, write down the step number and what you saw.

**Before you start**
- Open the link you were given. If it asks for an **access code**, type the code you were given.
- Everyone testing shares the same sample data. At the very start, scroll to the bottom of the sign in page and click **Reset demo** so you start fresh. Tell the other testers first.
- Every page has a pink **How does this page work?** link under the title. Click it any time you are unsure.

---

## Part 1: The client (sign in as Ada Nwosu)

Ada runs marketing at Northstar Wellness, a Leadhaus client.

**Signing in**
1. [ ] On the sign in page, click **Ada Nwosu**. You land on a page that says *Good morning (or afternoon/evening), Ada.*

**Dashboard (home)**
2. [ ] Click **How does this page work?** A short explanation opens. Click **Hide help** to close it.
3. [ ] You see four numbers: leads, cost per lead, ad spend and return on ad spend.
4. [ ] A pink box says **3 jobs waiting for you**. Click **Do this now** next to the first job. You go to *Creative approval*. Click your browser's back button.
5. [ ] Click **All reports** next to the chart. You go to *Reports*.

**Menu**
6. [ ] Click each item in the menu (left side, or along the top on a phone): Dashboard, Projects, Creative approval, Strategy, Reports, Onboarding, Documents, Messages, Billing. Each one opens a page with that name.

**Creative approval**
7. [ ] Open **Creative approval**. On *Testimonial cut*, click **V1**, then **V2**. The picture changes.
8. [ ] Click **Post** without typing. A red message asks you to type a comment first.
9. [ ] Type a comment and click **Post**. Your comment appears in the list.
10. [ ] Click **Request changes** without writing a note. A red message asks you to say what to change.
11. [ ] Write a note (for example *Use the October price*) and click **Request changes**. The creative moves to *Decided* and says *Revision requested*.
12. [ ] On *October offer static*, click **Approve**. It moves to *Decided* and says *Approved by Ada Nwosu*.

**Onboarding**
13. [ ] Open **Onboarding**. Under *October offer details*, click **Send** with the box empty. A red message appears.
14. [ ] Type an answer and click **Send**. The item gets a green tick.
15. [ ] Under *Google Tag Manager*, click **I have granted access**. It gets a green tick.
16. [ ] Under *Upload assets*, click **Upload** without choosing a file. A red message appears.
17. [ ] Click **Choose files**, pick any photo from your computer, then click **Upload**. The file name appears under *Uploaded*.
18. [ ] Go back to **Dashboard**. The pink jobs box is gone because all three jobs are done.

**Documents**
19. [ ] Open **Documents** and click **Service agreement**. The agreement opens.
20. [ ] Click **Print or save as PDF**. Your print window opens (you can cancel it). Click **Back to documents**.

**Messages**
21. [ ] Open **Messages**. Type a question in the *Message* box and click **Send message**. It appears in the list.

**Billing**
22. [ ] Open **Billing**. Under *To pay*, click **View invoice**. You see the amount and bank details.
23. [ ] Click **I have paid**. A message thanks you. Click **Back to billing**. The invoice says *Payment sent, awaiting confirmation*.

**Reports and Strategy**
24. [ ] Open **Reports**. Under *Previous reports*, click **Open** on the August report. It expands.
25. [ ] Open **Strategy**. You see the plan, goals and recommendations.

**Privacy checks (very important)**
26. [ ] Nowhere in Ada's pages should you see: other clients (Fika Foods), profit or margin numbers, freelancer fees, lead scores, or notes that start with *Internal*.
27. [ ] Click the **sign out** icon (bottom of the menu, or top right on a phone). You are back on the sign in page.

## Part 2: The Leadhaus team (sign in as Dare, Oma or Fareedah)

**Overview**
28. [ ] Click **Dare**. You see the team overview with revenue, profit, pipeline and outstanding invoices.
29. [ ] Under *Needs attention* you see Ada's question, her change request and *Northstar says INV-2026-011 is paid*. Click the payment line. You go to *Finance*.
30. [ ] *Recent activity* lists what Ada did in Part 1.

**Leads**
31. [ ] Open **Leads**. Type *meridian* in the search box and click **Search**. Only Meridian Health Group shows. Clear the box and click **Search** again.
32. [ ] Click **Add a lead**, fill in a company and contact name, click **Save lead**. It appears at the top as *New Lead*.
33. [ ] Click **Advance** on your new lead. It changes to *Contacted*.
34. [ ] Click **Lost** on *Brightpath Schools*. It changes to *Lost*.

**Clients**
35. [ ] Open **Clients**. Northstar shows a red **Needs reply** label on Ada's question, a yellow *Internal* box with margin and notes, and the photo Ada uploaded.
36. [ ] Type a reply in **Post to Northstar Wellness** and click **Post**. The *Needs reply* label disappears.
37. [ ] Type another message, tick **Internal note only**, click **Post**. It shows with an *Internal* label.

**Delivery**
38. [ ] Open **Delivery**. On *Retargeting static*, click **Send to client**. Its status becomes *Client review*.
39. [ ] Click **Add a task**, fill it in, pick a due date, click **Save task**. It appears in the list.
40. [ ] Click **Done** on any task. It disappears.
41. [ ] On *October performance report*, click **Publish**. It changes to *Published*.

**Finance**
42. [ ] Open **Finance**. *INV-2026-011* says *Client says paid*. Click **Mark paid**. It changes to *Paid*.
43. [ ] Click **Create an invoice**, fill in what it is for, the amount and a due date, then click **Create invoice**. It appears in the list.

**Team**
44. [ ] Open **Team**. You see Dare, Oma and Fareedah with their clients, tasks and leads.
45. [ ] Sign out.

## Part 3: Check the client sees the team's work

46. [ ] Sign in as **Ada Nwosu** again. The dashboard shows a new job: *Review Retargeting static*.
47. [ ] **Messages** shows the team's reply but NOT the internal note.
48. [ ] **Reports** now includes the October report.
49. [ ] **Billing** shows the new invoice, and INV-2026-011 is under *Payment history*.
50. [ ] Sign out, then sign in as **Zainab Bello**. You only see Fika Foods. Nothing about Northstar or Ada.

## Part 4: Team controls (what the team can change for a client)

51. [ ] Sign in as **Dare** and open **Settings**. Type *123* as the account number and click **Save settings**. A red message says account numbers have 10 digits.
52. [ ] Fix the account number (10 digits), change the bank name, type a message in **Announcement to all clients**, and click **Save settings**.
53. [ ] Open **Clients** and click **Manage portal** on Northstar Wellness.
54. [ ] In **Goals and KPIs**, change a *Now* number and click **Save goals**.
55. [ ] In a project, type a new progress number (for example 75), change a deliverable status, add a new deliverable with a due date, and click **Save project**.
56. [ ] In **Campaign status**, change *October offer launch* to *Live* and click **Save**.
57. [ ] Click **Ask the client for something**, type *TikTok access*, pick *Account access*, click **Add to their list**.
58. [ ] In **Portal settings**, pick a different account lead, untick **Strategy**, type a dashboard note, click **Save portal settings**.
59. [ ] Sign in as **Ada Nwosu**. Check: the pink announcement and the dashboard note are on her home page; *TikTok access* is in her jobs; Strategy is gone from her menu; Projects shows the new progress and deliverable; the invoice shows the new bank details.
60. [ ] As Ada, send a question on **Messages**. Sign in as **Oma**, open **Inbox**, pick Northstar, reply and click **Send**. The *Needs reply* label disappears. Sign back in as Ada: the reply is in her chat.

**Finish:** click **Reset demo** on the sign in page so the next tester starts fresh.

---

### What to report
For anything that did not work, send: the step number, what you clicked, what you expected, and what happened (a screenshot helps). Also note anything confusing, even if it worked.
